# Causey's Approach to Strategic Planning

## Core philosophy

A strategic plan that sits on a shelf is a plan that fails. Causey is built around one conviction: **when your team can see the plan, own their part, and track real progress, your mission moves faster.**

This belief comes from 25+ years of working alongside nonprofit leaders building, executing, and refreshing strategic plans.

## The START framework

Causey includes the **START Lite course** in every tier — a guided walkthrough of nonprofit strategic planning. The framework breaks plan creation into:

1. **Survey** — what's true now? Stakeholder input, environmental scan, internal assessment.
2. **Target** — where are you going? Vision, mission alignment, 3-5 year goals.
3. **Approach** — how will you get there? Strategies and initiatives.
4. **Roadmap** — when does it happen? Timelines, owners, milestones.
5. **Track** — are you on course? Metrics, regular updates, course correction.

The platform mirrors this structure: goals → strategies → action items → metrics, with owners and timelines on each.

## Plan structure in Causey

- **Goals** — broad outcomes ("Increase youth engagement").
- **Strategies** — how you'll pursue each goal ("Expand after-school programming to two new sites").
- **Action Items** — specific deliverables under each strategy, with owners and dates.
- **Metrics** — quantitative measures attached to goals (and trackable over time on charts).

## Built-in guidance

- **Templates** — common nonprofit plan structures (program-based, theory-of-change, balanced scorecard).
- **Examples** — real plan structures used by Causey customers.
- **Inline help** — context-sensitive guidance as you build.
- **START Lite course** — self-paced video lessons walking through each phase.

## Premium onboarding (Solid tier)

The Solid tier includes a **Guided Setup Call** with the Causey team to:

- Translate an existing plan (Word doc, PDF, spreadsheet) into Causey structure.
- Set up metric tracking and reporting cadence.
- Establish the team check-in rhythm (who updates what, when).

## When to use Causey vs. a strategic planning consultant

- **Causey alone:** existing plan that needs a home, or a team that wants to DIY plan creation with the START course.
- **Causey + consultant:** complex multi-stakeholder plan, board retreat facilitation needed, or a major strategic refresh.

The platform doesn't replace good facilitation — but it ensures the plan that comes out of the retreat doesn't die in a binder.
