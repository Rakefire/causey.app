# Causey: Strategic Planning Software for Nonprofits

## What it is

Causey is a SaaS platform that turns nonprofit strategic plans into daily action. Instead of letting a strategic plan sit in a Google Doc or PDF that nobody opens, Causey gives the plan a living home where every team member can see priorities, track action items, and report progress against goals and metrics.

**Core promise:** Walk into your next board meeting with confidence. Show funders measurable progress. Stop chasing your team for status updates.

## Who it's for

- **Primary:** Executive Directors, CEOs, Directors of Programs, and leadership teams at nonprofit organizations.
- **Use cases:** Strategic plan execution, board reporting, funder accountability, program management, accreditation reporting.
- **Org size:** From single-person planning teams (Basic tier) to large nonprofits with multiple stakeholders and complex priorities (Solid tier).
- **Common pain points:** plan-on-a-shelf syndrome, scrambling for updates before board meetings, disconnected goals and metrics, can't show measurable impact to funders.

## What makes Causey different

- **Built for nonprofits, not adapted from corporate software.** 25+ years of nonprofit strategic planning experience baked into the product.
- **Connects qualitative goals to quantitative metrics.** Your "increase community engagement" goal isn't just a paragraph — it's tied to numbers you can chart over time.
- **Automates the update-chasing.** Causey emails the right people on the right schedule asking for progress on their action items. Your team updates directly in the platform.
- **Strategic planning guidance is included.** Templates, examples, the START Lite course — built in, not sold as add-ons.
- **Unlimited users on every tier.** No per-seat pricing penalty for involving the whole team.

## Core capabilities

- **Plan, Presentation, and Timeline views** for organizing and presenting strategy.
- **Goals and Metrics tracking** with charts and reports.
- **Automated email reminders** to team members for status updates.
- **Comments** on goals, strategies, and action items for team discussion.
- **PDF export** for sharing plans with boards and stakeholders.
- **Document storage** for attachments, links, and notes.
- **Dashboard widgets** for funder/board-ready summaries.

## How it gets used

1. **Create the plan** — start from scratch with built-in guidance, or import an existing plan.
2. **Assign owners and timelines** — every goal, strategy, and action item has a person and a date.
3. **Track metrics** — connect quantitative measures to qualitative goals.
4. **Automated check-ins** — Causey emails team members on a schedule asking for updates.
5. **Report out** — PDF exports, dashboard views, and presentation mode for boards and funders.
