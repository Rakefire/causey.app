# Causey Pricing

Every tier includes **unlimited users**. No per-seat charges. Annual billing saves **16%**.

## Tiers

### Basic — $30/month or $300/year
- 1 active metric
- 256 MB document storage
- Plan, Presentation, and Timeline views
- PDF export
- Strategic planning guidance + START Lite course
- Email and KB support

**Best for:** Solo planners or 1-2 people managing the plan. Plan stays a reference, not actively updated by the whole team.

### Lite — $90/month or $900/year
- Up to 4 active metrics
- 500 MB storage
- **Automated email reminders** for team status updates
- **Comments** on goals/strategies/action items
- **Goal charts, metric reports, dashboard widgets**
- Basic Focus Mode
- Email support

**Best for:** Small to mid-size nonprofits where the whole team should own their part of the plan.

### Core — $130/month or $1,300/year
- Up to 15 active metrics
- 1 GB storage
- Super Focus Mode
- Filters and folders
- Everything in Lite

**Best for:** Mid-size nonprofits actively scaling their strategic execution; multiple programs, multiple owners.

### Solid — $275/month or $2,750/year
- Unlimited active metrics
- 5 GB storage
- Tags and custom fields
- Priority support
- **Guided Setup Call** included (premium onboarding)
- Everything in Core

**Best for:** Larger nonprofits with multiple stakeholder groups (board, funders, accreditors), complex strategic priorities.

## How to choose

- **Just need a clean home for your plan?** → Basic.
- **Want your whole team updating progress?** → Lite (this is the most common starting tier).
- **Multiple programs, lots of metrics?** → Core.
- **Reporting to multiple stakeholder groups, want guided onboarding?** → Solid.
