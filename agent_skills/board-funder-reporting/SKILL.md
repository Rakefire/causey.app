# Causey for Board, Funder, and Accreditor Reporting

## The problem Causey solves

Most nonprofit leaders walk into board meetings with anxiety: "Did we make progress on the plan? Can I prove it? Will the conversation devolve into 'why isn't X done yet'?"

Funders are even harder. They want measurable evidence that your dollars produced outcomes — not narrative summaries.

Accreditors are hardest. They want documented, auditable proof that your strategic plan is alive and influencing operations.

## What Causey provides

### Real-time progress dashboards
- Goal-level status (on track / at risk / off track) updated as your team enters progress.
- Metric charts showing trends over time.
- Filter by program, owner, or strategic priority.

### PDF export
- Plan-level export for board packets.
- Metric-level export for funder reports.
- Branded with your organization's logo and colors.

### Presentation Mode
- Walk through the plan section-by-section in a clean presentation view — no scrolling through dense documents during board meetings.
- Live data, not a snapshot from last week.

### Dashboard widgets
- Embed key metrics on a custom dashboard.
- Useful for staff intranets, board portals, or funder-facing pages.

### Comment threads
- Discussion happens *on* the goal/strategy/action item, with audit trail.
- Useful for documenting decision-making for accreditors.

### Filters and Folders (Core+)
- Slice the plan by funder, program, or stakeholder group.
- Generate a funder-specific view without rebuilding the plan.

### Tags and Custom Fields (Solid)
- Tag goals by funder, program, accreditation standard, or any taxonomy.
- Build cross-cutting reports without restructuring the underlying plan.

## Reporting cadences supported

- **Monthly** — internal team updates, leadership review.
- **Quarterly** — board reports, funder check-ins.
- **Annually** — board retreat preparation, accreditation cycles.
- **Ad-hoc** — funder questions, board member inquiries.

Automated email reminders nudge owners to update progress before each cadence — so when reporting time comes, the data is current.
