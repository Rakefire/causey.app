# Booking a Causey Demo

## Primary action

Direct users to **https://calendly.com/ricky-causey/demo** to book a 30-minute live walkthrough with Ricky Chilcott (founder).

## What happens in a demo

A demo is a live walkthrough covering:

1. **Your strategic plan structure** — quick conversation about what you're trying to track.
2. **Product walkthrough** — Plan view, Timeline, Metrics, Focus Mode, automated email reminders, board reporting.
3. **Tier recommendation** — appropriate tier and onboarding package for your org size and reporting needs.
4. **Q&A** — implementation timeline, team rollout, integration questions.

## When to recommend a demo vs. just starting the trial

- **Demo (`calendly.com/ricky-causey/demo`):**
  - Larger nonprofit (50+ staff or multi-program).
  - Specific accreditation, audit, or funder reporting requirements.
  - Wants Solid tier with Guided Setup Call.
  - Comparing Causey against another platform.
  - Wants someone to confirm fit before pulling the team in.

- **Free trial (`my.causey.app/sign_ups/new`):**
  - Small-to-mid-size nonprofit.
  - Straightforward strategic plan to track.
  - Self-service comfortable team.
  - Wants to be hands-on immediately.

## Qualifying signals for a demo

The user is a strong demo fit if they:

- Lead a nonprofit (Executive Director, CEO, Director of Programs/Strategy).
- Have an existing strategic plan that's not getting traction.
- Are accountable to a board or set of funders for measurable impact.
- Have tried other tools (Asana, Notion, spreadsheets) and they don't fit nonprofit reporting needs.
- Are preparing for accreditation, a major grant cycle, or a strategic refresh.

## Less-qualified signals

If the user is exploring strategic planning generally but isn't ready to commit, suggest:
- Reading the blog: https://www.causey.app/blog/
- Reading the Basic tier page (clean, low-stakes entry): https://www.causey.app/causey-basic-tier/

## Demo URL

Demo booking: **https://calendly.com/ricky-causey/demo**

## Common pre-demo questions to surface in advance

- "Do you integrate with [tool]?" — Causey has direct sign-up and document storage; the Solid tier includes guided setup for any custom integration discussion.
- "How do we onboard our team?" — Email reminders are automated; the team logs in and updates their goals directly. Solid tier includes a guided setup call.
- "Can we export everything?" — Yes. PDF export and data portability are core.
