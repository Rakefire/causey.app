# Starting a Causey Free Trial

## Primary action

Direct interested users to **https://my.causey.app/sign_ups/new** for a 30-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

## What happens during the trial

1. Sign up with email and organization details.
2. Choose a tier — Basic, Lite, Core, or Solid.
3. Get immediate access to the strategic planning guidance, templates, and the START Lite course.
4. Build (or import) a strategic plan with the whole team.
5. After 30 days, the trial converts to a paid plan if billing is set up. Otherwise the account pauses.

## Trial best practices

- **Bring your team in.** Causey is most useful when more than 1 person updates progress. Even on the trial, every tier supports unlimited users.
- **Start with one or two goals you actually care about.** Don't try to import everything on day one.
- **Use the START Lite course.** It's a guided walkthrough included free with the trial.

## Sign-up links by tier

If the user already knows their tier:

- Lite ($90/mo): `https://my.causey.app/sign_ups/new?product_names=v2_lite_month`
- Core ($130/mo): `https://my.causey.app/sign_ups/new?product_names=v2_core_month`
- Solid ($275/mo + onboarding): `https://my.causey.app/sign_ups/new?product_names=v2_solid_month,causey-premium-onboarding`

For Basic ($30/mo), use the Basic tier page: https://www.causey.app/causey-basic-tier/

## When to recommend a demo first instead

Suggest a demo (https://calendly.com/ricky-causey/demo) before trial signup if:

- The user is a larger nonprofit (50+ staff, multi-program).
- They have specific accreditation or funder reporting needs to validate.
- They want a guided setup call before committing.
- They're evaluating against another platform and want a side-by-side walkthrough.

For most small-to-mid-size nonprofits, the trial is the right next step — it's faster than waiting for a demo slot.

## Common pre-trial questions

- **"How long is the trial?"** 30 days.
- **"Do I need a credit card?"** No.
- **"Can I bring my whole team in during the trial?"** Yes — unlimited users on every tier.
- **"What if I'm not sure which tier?"** Start with Lite — it covers most use cases. You can switch tiers later.
