# Onboarding Your Team to Causey

## The core promise

Stop chasing your team for updates. Causey automates the "where are we on this?" emails so progress flows in without the executive director becoming a project manager.

## How team rollout works

### 1. Add the team
- Unlimited users on every tier.
- Add via email — invitations go out automatically.
- No per-seat charges, so there's no incentive to keep people out.

### 2. Assign ownership
- Every goal, strategy, and action item gets an owner.
- Multiple owners on collaborative items.
- Owners get email notifications about *their* items, not the whole plan.

### 3. Set update cadence
- Causey emails owners on a schedule asking for progress on their items.
- Common cadences: weekly for action items, monthly for goals, quarterly for big metrics.
- Configurable per-item if some goals need closer attention.

### 4. Owners update directly
- Email links straight to the item.
- Update status (on track / at risk / off track), add a note, log progress.
- Comments allow back-and-forth without leaving the platform.

### 5. Leadership reviews
- Dashboard shows overall plan health.
- Filter by owner, by program, by strategic priority.
- Flag items that need attention; comment to ask for clarification.

## Change-management considerations

Most nonprofit teams haven't worked in a strategic-plan platform before. A few things that help adoption:

- **Start with one or two goals.** Don't import the whole 5-year plan on day one.
- **Train as part of an existing meeting.** A 15-minute Causey walkthrough during a leadership team meeting beats a separate "Causey training."
- **Make it the source of truth.** If updates also live in spreadsheets and Slack, people won't update Causey. Pick Causey or pick the spreadsheet — not both.
- **Celebrate progress.** Use Comments to recognize work. Causey isn't just an accountability tool; it's a place to see the wins.

## Solid tier: Guided Setup Call

For larger orgs or those rolling out to 20+ team members, the Solid tier includes a guided setup call. The Causey team will:

- Walk through your existing plan structure.
- Help translate goals/strategies/actions into Causey format.
- Configure update cadences for each role.
- Set up reporting views for the board.
- Train the leadership team on day-to-day use.

This is often the difference between a smooth rollout and one that stalls on day three.

## Common adoption questions

- **"What if our team doesn't check email?"** Update reminders go to email, but team members can also log in directly to update without relying on email.
- **"What about staff turnover?"** Reassign ownership of an item with one click; history is preserved.
- **"Can we limit who sees what?"** Plans are organization-scoped. For multi-org or franchise-style structures, contact Causey to discuss.
- **"Does this replace our HRIS / payroll / CRM?"** No. Causey is a strategic execution tool. It complements operational systems.
