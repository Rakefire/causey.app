Causey Basic

$30/month or $300/year · Unlimited users

Basic is a clean, structured home for your strategic plan — a real step up from spreadsheets and shared docs. If all you need is a place to organize your plan, track goals and metrics, and reference progress over time, Basic does exactly that at a price that's easy to justify.

What you get

  • Plan, Presentation, and Timeline views to organize and present your strategy

  • Goals and Metrics tracking (1 active metric) to measure what matters

  • Document storage (256 MB) for attachments, links, and notes

  • PDF export to share your plan with your board or stakeholders

  • Strategic planning guidance and the START Lite course included

  • Email and knowledge base support

Who Basic is for

Basic is a good fit if one or two people manage the plan and you don't need the rest of your team updating progress directly in the platform. It's practical, affordable, and far better than a plan buried in a Google Doc.

When to choose Lite instead

If you want your whole team involved — not just viewing the plan, but actively owning their piece of it — Lite ($90/month) adds the features that make that possible:

  • Automated email reminders that ask your team to update their goals and metrics on a regular schedule — so you stop chasing people for updates

  • Comments so your team can discuss progress directly on goals, strategies, and action items

  • Goal charts, metric reports, and widgets for richer reporting to your board and funders

For most organizations where more than one or two people touch the plan, Lite pays for itself in time saved.
