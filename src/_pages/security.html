Report a vulnerability

If you think you've found a security problem in Causey, email security@causey.app. Tell us what you found and how to reproduce it. We'll acknowledge your report within 3 business days and keep you updated as we look into it.

Please give us time to fix the problem before you share it publicly. By default we ask for 90 days from your report, and we'll agree on a different timeline with you if a fix needs one.

We don't run a bug bounty program, so we can't pay for reports.

If you act in good faith and follow the rules below, we won't pursue legal action against you for your research.

In scope

  • https://my.causey.app/
  • https://www.causey.app/

Out of scope

  • Denial-of-service attacks
  • Spam or social engineering aimed at our team or customers
  • Physical attacks on our people, offices or equipment
  • Problems caused by how a customer has configured their own organization
  • Automated scanner output with no demonstrated impact
  • Third-party services (Stripe, Render, Cloudflare and similar). Report problems with them to the vendor.

Ground rules

  • Test only against your own accounts and data.
  • Don't run automated scans that flood the service.

How we protect your data

  • All traffic to the Causey app is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.
  • Access inside an organization is controlled by role.
  • Our databases are continuously replicated and snapshotted nightly to off-site storage.

For how we handle personal information, read our Privacy Policy. For payment card handling, see our PCI compliance statement (PDF).
