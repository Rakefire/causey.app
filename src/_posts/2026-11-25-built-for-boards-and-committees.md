Most strategic plans aren't carried out by staff alone. A goal might belong to the Finance Committee. An objective might be shared with a partner organization. And board members open the plan a few times a year, usually the night before a meeting, long after they've forgotten the password they chose in the spring.

Causey used to assume that every name on a plan was a person with an inbox. Some of you worked around that by inviting a committee as if it were a member, with a shared email address. It worked, mostly, but it wasn't what you needed.

This month's changes are for the boards, committees and occasional visitors who are part of your plan.

## **Roles: Let a Committee Own a Goal**

A role stands in for a committee, a staff position or a partner organization. It can be a champion or a team member like anyone else, but it never signs in and never gets email.

To add one, go to Settings, open Members, and choose "Add a Role." All it needs is a name. Putting the person who holds it in the name helps, as in "Finance Committee (Chair: Hannah)." When the chair changes, you rename the role, and every goal it champions stays exactly where it was.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: Settings > Members, showing a role row beside people, 1400x900 -->

Roles show up in the champion and team pickers with a square avatar and the word "Role" under the name, so nobody mistakes them for a person. You can give a role a logo, too.

If you already set up a committee as a member who never finished signing up, you don't have to start over. Choose "Make this a role" from that member's menu. It keeps every goal and item they're assigned to and removes the email address.

## **When Someone Leaves**

People move on, and their name shouldn't stay on goals nobody is working on. When you archive or remove someone who is still a champion or team member on your active plan, Causey now offers to move those assignments to another member or a role in one step. Past plans keep their record of who did the work at the time.

Archiving and restoring now live in each member's menu, and archived members are hidden from the Members page until you choose to show them.

## **Groups**

The new Groups page in Settings lets you say who belongs together, like your board, your staff or a committee. Someone can be in more than one group. On the Members page you can then show just one group, or everyone who isn't in a group yet.

Groups are labels for your own organizing. They don't change what anyone can see or edit.

## **Signing In Without a Password**

The sign-in page now has an "Email me a sign-in link" button. Enter your email address and Causey sends a link that signs you in. Each link works once and expires after 15 minutes. Passwords still work for anyone who prefers them.

<!-- SCREENSHOT: The redesigned sign-in page with "Email me a sign-in link", 1400x900 -->

A few related changes came with it:

- When someone accepts an invitation, choosing a password is now optional. They can confirm their email and sign in with links from then on.
- You stay signed in until you've been away for 120 days, up from two weeks. A board member who checks in once a quarter won't have to sign in every time.
- If you'd rather use only a password, check "Sign in with my password only" in your account settings and Causey won't email you links.
- Changing your password now asks for your current one, and signs you out on your other devices.
- The sign-in pages have a new, simpler design.

## **Viewers Can Read Comments**

Board members often have Viewer access, and until now that meant they couldn't see the conversation happening on the plan. Viewers can now read comments, though adding and replying is still up to the people who edit the plan.

## **Available Today**

Roles, Groups and emailed sign-in links are on every Causey plan. Comments are part of the Lite, Core and Solid plans. You'll find more on all of this at [help.causey.app](https://help.causey.app/){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.
