A strategic plan can hold a lot of useful information, but having it all in one place does not always make it easy to interpret.

When it is time to prepare for a board meeting, review progress with leadership, or understand what changed over the last quarter, you may still need to sort through goals, strategies, metrics, actions, and updates before the bigger picture becomes clear.

AI can help with that, especially when it has the right context. Causey’s AI Summary creates a text-based version of your plan that you can copy into the AI tool your organization already uses.

Instead of explaining your plan piece by piece, you can start asking questions about the information already there. You might ask for a summary of recent progress, identify goals that need attention, highlight accomplishments for a board update, or prepare questions for a strategy meeting.

The purpose is not to have AI make strategic decisions for you. It is to make the information your team is already maintaining easier to understand and use.

## **Why Causey Does Not Build AI Into the Product**

Organizations have different requirements for how they use AI and what information they are comfortable sharing with it. Some use ChatGPT, while others use Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or another internally approved tool.

Rather than require every Causey customer to use the same AI provider, we chose to make plan information easy to extract and use with the tool that already works for your organization.

This also gives your team more control over privacy, security, and internal AI policies. Causey remains the place where your strategic plan is maintained, while the AI tool gives you another way to explore the information in it.

## **The Quality of the Output Depends on the Quality of the Plan**

AI can only work with the information you provide, so its usefulness depends in part on how current your plan is.

When goals, metrics, actions, and progress updates are kept up to date, the AI has better context to work from. That makes it easier to understand what has changed, where progress is being made, and what may need attention next.

This is one more reason an active strategic plan is more useful than one that is only revisited at reporting time. AI does not replace the work of maintaining or interpreting the plan, but it can make the information already there easier to use.

If you want to try this with your own plan, see our guide on [how to generate and use an AI Summary](https://help.causey.app/).
