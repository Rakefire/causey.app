A strategic plan gathers paperwork. Board minutes, grant reports, a PDF of the plan itself, the spreadsheet behind a metric. Causey's Documents page is where all of that lives, and it hadn't kept up.

Every category was stacked down the page as its own collapsible card. Folders opened in a pop-up window. And if you wanted to send a colleague to a particular file, the best you could do was tell them where to click.

So we rebuilt it.

## **Categories on the Left, One Panel on the Right**

Your categories are now listed down the left side, each with how many documents it holds. Click one and it fills a single panel on the right, with its name, a plain count like "4 documents, 1 folder," and its tags.

![The Documents page, with categories listed on the left and Board Packets open in the panel beside them](/uploads/documents-categories.jpg){: width="1400" height="600"}

Everything you can do to a category is in two menus at the top of that panel. "Add" creates an attachment, a note or a link. The ⋯ menu edits the category, adds a folder or deletes it. You can drag categories in the list to put them in the order you want, and the tag filter narrows the list to the categories you care about.

On a phone, the category list turns into a row you can scroll sideways above the panel.

## **Folders Open in Place, With a Link You Can Share**

Click a folder and it opens right in the panel, with a breadcrumb above it (Category › Folder › Subfolder) so you always know where you are. When you add or edit something inside a folder, you stay in that folder.

Every category and folder now has its own web address. That means your browser's Back button takes you where you were, you can bookmark the folder you open every week, and you can paste a link into an email and your colleague lands in the same spot. They still need access to your organization's documents to open it.

## **Move Documents by Dragging Them**

To file a document, drag it onto a folder. To move it up a level, drag it onto a name in the breadcrumb. If you'd rather not drag, each document's ⋯ menu has a "Move to" list with the same choices.

## **Easier to Scan**

Each document is now a single line: an icon tinted by type, then the name, when it was added and updated, and its size. PDFs are red, Word files blue, PowerPoint orange, spreadsheets green, links teal, notes gray and folders brown, so you can spot the file you want before you read a word.

![A folder open in the Documents panel, with its breadcrumb and one-line rows whose icons are tinted by file type](/uploads/documents-folder-breadcrumb.jpg){: width="1400" height="720"}

The same rows appear in the Documents sections inside your plan, under focus areas, goals and action items, and those sections now use the same "Add" menu.

## **Drop Files to Upload Them**

Anywhere Causey asks for a file, you can now drag one in from your computer, or click to choose one as before. The attachment form takes up to 20 files at once. Each becomes its own document, named after the file, and they all share the folder and tags you choose.

You can also drop files from your computer straight onto a category, a folder or a plan item's Documents section, and the attachment form opens with them ready to go.

Uploads start as soon as you choose or drop a file, with a progress bar, and keep going while you fill in the rest of the form. If your network blocks that, Causey sends the file the usual way when you save.

## **Available Today**

The new Documents page is on every Causey plan. Folders are part of the Core and Solid plans, and attachments count toward your plan's storage, which you'll find on our [pricing page](/pricing/).

If anything on the new page slows you down, or you miss something from the old one, let us know at [help@causey.app](mailto:help@causey.app). We read every note.
