Picture a quarterly check-in. Four people have the plan open on their laptops. One updates a goal's status, another records last month's numbers, and somebody asks, "Is that in yet?" Everyone reloads, scrolls back down to where they were, and reopens the section they had collapsed.

Strategic plans are group work, and Causey should feel like it. Over the past several weeks we've made two changes so it does: you can see who else is on the page with you, and their changes show up on your screen as they make them.

## **See Who's Here**

When someone else has the same page open, their photo or initials appear in a small row of circles at the top of the page. Hover over a circle to see who it is.

If they have an edit form open, a ring appears around their circle and the tooltip tells you what they're working on, such as "with Edit Goal open." That's a helpful nudge before two people start rewriting the same goal.

![Three teammates’ circles at the top of the Plan view, with the tooltip Maria Delgado is also on this page, with Edit Goal open](/uploads/live-presence-tooltip.jpg){: width="1400" height="257"}

When a lot of people are on one page, Causey shows the first few and folds the rest into a "+3" style chip, and hovering over it lists everyone.

You'll see this on the pages people work on together, like your plan, Metrics and Documents. Reports and Settings are usually one person's work, so they don't show it.

## **Changes Appear as They Happen**

On the Plan and Actions views, when a teammate edits an item, adds a new one, posts a progress update, reorders the plan or records a measurement, your page updates on its own. The Metrics page does the same, in both the list and the chart view.

Just as important, you keep your place: the page stays where you scrolled it, the items you collapsed stay collapsed, and your filters stay applied.

## **Your Typing Is Safe**

Causey won't refresh a page while you're typing on it. Instead, you'll see a notice that says "Someone else updated this plan" (or "these metrics") with a "Show latest" button, and you can click it when you're done.

![The Someone else updated this plan notice with its Show latest button, at the top of the Plan view](/uploads/live-plan-updated-notice.jpg){: width="1400" height="257"}

If you're working in a pop-up form, like editing a goal, the form stays open with everything you've typed while the page behind it updates.

There's one more case. If an administrator changes your access while you have Causey open, your open tabs update and show a note saying your access changed, so you're not left working from permissions you no longer have.

## **Made for Meetings**

A lot of a plan's value comes from people looking at it together: a staff meeting where each lead updates their goals, or a board meeting with the plan up on the screen while someone records decisions. Now everyone in that meeting sees the same plan, and sees it change as the work gets recorded.

As always, if something doesn't behave the way you'd expect, please tell us at [help@causey.app](mailto:help@causey.app). We read every note.
