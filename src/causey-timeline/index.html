Causey Timeline Generator

The Causey Timeline Generator is a free, browser-based tool that turns a nonprofit's strategic plan into a clean, shareable visual timeline. Add your focus areas, goals, and action items with start and end dates, and the tool lays everything out so you and your team can see how the work sequences across the months and quarters ahead.

Most strategic plans live in a spreadsheet or a long document where it is hard to see how priorities overlap or compete for attention. A visual timeline makes that obvious. You can spot a quarter that is over-committed, a goal with no owner, or a milestone that should move earlier so the rest of the plan can land on schedule.

This tool is designed for nonprofit leaders, executive directors, board members, and strategy consultants who need a fast way to communicate a plan without buying project-management software. Use it on its own, or pair it with Causey, our strategic planning platform built for mission-driven organizations.

What you can do with the timeline generator

  • Add focus areas, goals, and action items with start and end dates
  • See your full plan as a horizontal Gantt-style timeline
  • Share a link with your team or board for review
  • Export the timeline to include in board packets and reports
  • Adjust dates and ownership as the plan evolves

If you want a permanent home for your strategic plan with reminders, progress updates, and team accountability, explore Causey's plans.
